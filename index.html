Scouting Report

Market map

Three pipes splitting — shared listing databases (MLSs), brokerage groups, and private networks. AI connectors rising. A permission layer deciding who gets in.

Where we play

The gaps between the pipes — consent, compliance, permissions that travel with the data. No MLS permission needed to start.

In one line

Brokerages just built their own pipe, permissions are becoming the product — and no Bay Area MLS has joined either. Yet.
